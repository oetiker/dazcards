## Twemoji graphics

The pictures in `public/starter/`, `public/avatars/`, `public/templates/` and
`public/star.svg` are from **Twemoji** (© Twitter, Inc and other contributors;
maintained at https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji), licensed under
**CC BY 4.0** – https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

They were taken unmodified from the `@twemoji/svg` 15.0.0 package and renamed
(e.g. `1f436.svg` → `hund.svg`).

The CC BY 4.0 graphics are bundled as separate works next to the GPL-licensed
program code. The attribution above must be shipped with the app; it is also
shown in the adult area (TODO: about page).
